A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before the
first sleepover.

A little preparation.
A much happier goodbye.

What should I pack for my dog?

Their usual food in labeled portions, a leash or harness, and a familiar blanket or favorite toy. Include feeding instructions, care notes, and your contact details when arranging an actual stay.

Can you follow their usual meal routine?

That's the idea. Bring their regular food and tell the team about portions, timing, and allergies. Keeping meals familiar helps them settle in. Any special arrangements should be confirmed with the hotel before arrival.

It's their first stay. Will they be okay?

We plan for a gentle introduction, time to sniff around, and a quiet place to settle. A short introductory visit can help you and the team understand what makes your dog comfortable.

Does every dog join group play?

No. Play should match their personality and comfort level. Some love a small group; others prefer one-on-one attention and quieter walks. Share their preferences before their stay.

Can two dogs share a room?

The Garden Hangout demo accommodates up to two dogs from the same household at the displayed room rate. Sharing is best for dogs who are already comfortable resting together.

What about medication or special care?

Tell the team before making a real reservation so they can discuss whether the care needed can be accommodated. This demo does not confirm medical support or special-care availability.

Does this booking reserve a real room?

No. This is a booking demo with sample room rates and illustrative, AI-generated photography. No payment is taken, no availability is checked, and no reservation is sent.
