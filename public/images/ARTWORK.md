## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production transparent PNG asset sheet for Pawhaus dog hotel hero, using image 1 as reference for a fluffy white shaggy dog in a cap and image 2 for a golden cream corgi with upright ears. Soft premium 3D animation-movie render, very cute, plush detailed fur, warm studio lighting. Two separate dogs side by side, white dog LEFT and corgi RIGHT, each centered within its own half with ample transparent space between them. Front view, heads looking directly at viewer. Show each dog from cap/ears to upper torso, each with TWO front paws held forward at the SAME horizontal baseline near bottom, hanging over an imaginary ledge (DO NOT render any ledge). White dog wears muted butter yellow baseball cap and transparent pale yellow oversized rectangular glasses, light powder blue vest. Corgi wears round butter yellow glasses with CLEAR transparent lenses, eyes visible, small powder blue collar and tiny round gold tag, smiling tongue slightly out. Both have big brown eyes visibly gazing forward through clear lenses. Keep characteristic silhouettes of references. Simplify streetwear into tasteful hotel vacation accessories. Composition: full ears/cap not cropped, dogs separate with NO overlap, both paws visible, nothing behind them, no props, no text, no floor, no shadow on background, actual transparent background. Output wide landscape sheet.

## Directional heads

Built-in imagegen produced dog-head-turns.png, a transparent 1983 by 793 pixel atlas with five head portraits per character. The dogs.png asset was the identity and style reference. The generation requested five equal columns, two rows, white dog above corgi, heads only, and left 32 degrees, forward, right 32 degrees, up 22 degrees, and down 20 degrees poses. Accessories, soft 3D fur, and expressions were preserved. The runtime feathers cell borders to suppress neighboring portrait edges.
